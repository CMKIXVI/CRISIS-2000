U.S. Treasuries record their worst quarter since 1994

Reuters reports the U.S. 10-year Treasury yield rose 87.1bp in Q3 to a 16-year high of 5.31%, while the 30-year reached 5.65%, its highest since 2002. European, Australian, South Korean and Japanese bonds also declined.

Bank of England flags interconnected sovereign, credit and geopolitical vulnerabilities
Bank of England · 30 Sep 2026 · Financial stability
● MATERIAL

The FPC says the re-escalation of the Middle East conflict and higher oil, gas and refined-product prices have contributed to sustained sovereign-yield increases across advanced economies. It says the financial system has remained resilient so far, while leverage and interconnected vulnerabilities remain watchpoints.

Open Bank of England evidence →
RBA identifies persistent sovereign-bond and repo-market amplification risks
Reserve Bank of Australia · Oct 2026 · Financial Stability Review
● WATCH

The RBA says elevated government debt, higher yields and greater participation by leveraged non-banks could amplify a stress event through sovereign and closely linked repo markets. At the same time, it says aggregate corporate credit spreads remain low by historical standards.

Open RBA evidence →
Bank of Canada: markets remain resilient despite higher vulnerability
Bank of Canada · 2026 Financial Stability Report · Financial markets
● CONTAINED

The Bank of Canada reports periods of increased volatility and reduced liquidity, especially in energy, but says markets have generally remained resilient and credit spreads remain compressed. This is a key negative-confirmation input for the current “not established contagion” state.
